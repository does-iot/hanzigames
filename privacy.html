感谢你使用「嘟嘟找别字」（以下简称"本应用"）。我们非常重视你和孩子的隐私。

本应用面向儿童设计，正因如此我们不收集任何数据，符合 COPPA、GDPR-K 等儿童隐私保护的最小化原则。

Privacy Policy — "DuDu: Spot the Hanzi"

Effective date: June 13, 2026

Thank you for using "DuDu: Spot the Hanzi" (the "App"). We take your and your child's privacy seriously.

1. We collect no personal information

The App runs entirely on your local device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information (name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, device identifiers, usage behavior, or statistics).

2. No network, no third parties

The App runs fully offline with no server or backend; contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or SDKs; and contains no in-app browser or external links.

3. Game data

Scores and levels exist only in memory during the session and are cleared on close. They are never written to disk or uploaded.

4. Children's privacy

The App is designed for children. We collect no data at all, in line with COPPA and GDPR-K data minimization.

5. Updates

If a future version changes functionality, we will update this policy and post the new effective date.
