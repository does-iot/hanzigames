# Privacy Policy — "DuDu: Spot the Hanzi"

**Effective date: June 13, 2026**

Thank you for using "DuDu: Spot the Hanzi" (the "App"). We take your and your child's privacy seriously. This policy explains how the App handles information.

## 1. We collect no personal information
The App is an offline game that runs entirely on your local device. We do **not collect, store, transmit, or share** any personal information, including but not limited to:
- Identity information such as name, email, or phone number;
- Device data such as location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone;
- Device identifiers, usage behavior, analytics, or statistics.

## 2. No network, no third parties
- The App **runs fully offline** and has no server or backend.
- The App contains **no** third-party advertising, analytics, or software development kits (SDKs).
- The App contains **no** in-app browser or external links.

## 3. Game data
Scores, levels, and similar gameplay data exist only in memory during the current session and are cleared when the App closes. They are **never written to disk or uploaded**.

## 4. Children's privacy
The App is designed for children. Precisely for this reason we **collect no data at all**, in line with children's privacy requirements such as COPPA and GDPR-K (data minimization).

## 5. Updates to this policy
If a future version changes functionality (e.g., adds cloud saves), we will update this policy and post the new effective date on this page.
